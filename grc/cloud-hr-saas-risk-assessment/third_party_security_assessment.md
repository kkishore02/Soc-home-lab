# Third-Party Security Assessment

## Supplier Review Areas

### Security Governance
- Request security-policy overview and named security contacts.
- Confirm independent assurance such as ISO 27001 or equivalent evidence where applicable.

### Identity & Access
- Confirm MFA support for privileged users.
- Confirm SSO / federation support.
- Review RBAC and access-review capabilities.

### Vulnerability Management
- Ask how vulnerabilities are identified, prioritised and remediated.
- Request a high-level independent penetration-test summary where appropriate.

### Incident Response
- Confirm notification process and contractual timelines.
- Define escalation contacts and shared response responsibilities.

### Data Protection
- Identify data locations and subprocessors.
- Confirm encryption in transit and at rest.
- Review retention and deletion processes.

### Business Continuity
- Confirm service availability targets.
- Review recovery objectives and customer data-export options.

## Assessment Outcome

Proceed only after critical identity/access controls and high-priority supplier-assurance gaps are addressed or formally risk-accepted by an authorised owner.
