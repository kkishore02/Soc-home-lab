# Privacy / DPIA Screening

The fictional HR SaaS scenario involves employee personal data and may include sensitive employment information.

## Screening Questions

- What employee data is processed?
- Is special-category data involved?
- Where is the data stored?
- Who are the subprocessors?
- Is automated decision-making used?
- What are the retention periods?
- Are international transfers involved?
- What access controls and audit logs exist?

## Initial Outcome

A formal privacy review should be completed before implementation. A DPIA may be required depending on the exact categories of data, processing scale, monitoring and automated decision-making.

This document demonstrates security/privacy screening concepts only and is **not legal advice**.
